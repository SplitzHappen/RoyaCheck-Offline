Step 06

Evidence and limitations

Frozen model

Stage 7D A0 FP32 ONNX, with the fixed Stage 7C operating thresholds.

Internal evidence

On the frozen BRACOL internal holdout, 136 target-class leaves were eligible for target-class routing. The model produced 106 confident target-class outputs and 104 of those 106 were correct. The visible-rust route captured 66 of 95 rust leaves, while 27 of 95 rust leaves were routed to not sure and 2 of 95 rust leaves were routed to no visible rust. Other-condition false-visible-rust routing occurred for 7 of 117 other-condition leaves.

Claim ceiling

Those numbers are internal-holdout evidence only. They are not field validation, not RoCoLe external-transfer evidence, and not challenge-set evidence.

Safety

RoyaCheck does not diagnose a plant, select treatment, recommend pesticide use, explain falling yields, or replace qualified human review. It does not verify that an image is a coffee leaf.

Localization

This technical MVP includes a local-language fixed-string scaffold. Actual Lugisu/Lumasaba wording is pending fluent human validation. Validated localized usability has not been established.
