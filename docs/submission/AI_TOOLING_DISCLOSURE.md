# AI / Tooling Disclosure

This disclosure records how AI tools were used in the RoyaCheck Offline hackathon build. It is intended to support transparent review of the public submission, not to assign legal authorship to any AI system.

## Owner and accountability

José Antonio Tamburini Martínez is the sole human entrant, product owner, final decision-maker, presenter, and accountable author of the submission.

## AI-assisted development

Repository code, tests, documentation, pull-request bodies, evidence summaries, and repository-maintenance text may have been drafted, edited, reviewed, or organized with AI assistance under the owner's direction.

Commits authored through connected GitHub tooling under the owner's GitHub account may therefore contain AI-generated or AI-edited content. Commit authorship should not be read as a representation that every line was manually drafted without AI assistance.

ChatGPT / Vale acted as the primary AI builder and integrator. Claude acted as an independent adversarial auditor / red-team reviewer and did not serve as the default co-builder.

## Human decisions

The owner made the substantive project decisions, including:

- selecting RoyaCheck Offline as the product concept;
- selecting the browser-local, offline-capable coffee-leaf observation workflow;
- approving the MobileNetV3-Small / ONNX / ONNX Runtime Web model path;
- accepting residual pretrained-artifact licensing risk subject to disclosure;
- approving the human-final authority design, where the model proposes and the human signs the local record;
- deciding that unverified local-language strings must not be claimed as validated Lugisu/Lumasaba;
- preserving explicit non-claims around field validation, diagnosis, treatment, pesticide advice, WBG endorsement, production readiness, and replacement of qualified human review.

## Evidence and limits

AI assistance was used to prepare tests and evidence documents, but public claims must remain limited to the evidence actually run and recorded in the repository.

No AI system is claimed as an entrant, co-author, legal rights holder, accountable decision-maker, or substitute for the owner's final judgment.
